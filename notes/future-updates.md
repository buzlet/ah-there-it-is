# Future updates — parking lot

This branch is a durable place for ideas, follow-up improvements, experiments,
and possible future batches that are not part of the currently issued work.

## Rules

- Notes here are **not** requirements for an active batch unless they are later
  promoted into a normal issued task/design document.
- Do not use this branch as a replacement for an issuance SHA or for current
  `main`.
- Prefer short entries with context, motivation, risks, and the condition that
  would justify implementation.
- Keep speculative ideas separate from accepted decisions.
- When an item becomes real work, move/refine it into the normal planning/design
  and v9 issuance flow rather than implementing directly from this list.
- Preserve useful history; normally append or edit deliberately instead of
  periodically wiping the file.
- Record experiments and their outcomes in `notes/experiments.md`, including enough
  context to reproduce them later.
