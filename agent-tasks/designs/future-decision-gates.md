# Future decision gates

Only unresolved decisions are kept active here.

Resolved quantity/physical-instance semantics are recorded in:
`agent-tasks/designs/quantity-physical-instance-decision.md`.

## Correction / undo

Preferred principle: corrections use compensating mutations/Events rather than rewriting history. Generic Undo UX still requires a decision.

## Hard delete / purge

No ordinary hard delete. Destructive purge would require explicit privacy/product semantics.

## Trace retention

Decide raw agent trace retention/purge policy before long-term real-data accumulation at scale.

## Automated backups

Scheduling, retention, off-machine destination and encryption remain future operational decisions.

## Remote access / multi-user

Before LAN/Internet or household sharing, decide authentication, ownership, actor attribution, permissions and visibility.

## Optional integrations

Voice, images, QR/barcodes and external chat integrations remain separate stages.

## Provider/model promotion

Provider choice remains outside domain architecture. Future promotion may consider clarification rate, cost, latency, privacy and prompt versioning.
